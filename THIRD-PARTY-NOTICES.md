# Third-party notices

The installable BookStack module contains locally bundled open-source image decoders. They are loaded only when a matching image preview is requested.

- `@stacksjs/ts-heic` 0.0.2 is distributed under the MIT License. It is a pure TypeScript decoder without WebAssembly or runtime dependencies. Source: <https://github.com/stacksjs/ts-heic>.
- `utif2` 4.1.0 is distributed under the MIT License. Source: <https://github.com/photopea/UTIF.js>.

The license texts are copied into `licenses/` in the installable module. The original image attachment is never modified by conversion for display.
